## How logging works

- Logs to a 24-hour timeline, not Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner. Defaults to the current hour; the calendar icon changes it.
- + on a row adds the remembered serving straight to the plate and clears search (multi-add).
- Tapping a row opens a Food Detail View that shows the food's effect on your day; cancel or confirm.
- The plate stays visible in every mode (minified at the top), and Log Foods logs all of it at once.
- Speed mode returns to search after each add; context mode shows the plate instead.

## Take for NutriSA

- Mode row (Scan, Search, AI, Quick Add) under the top bar.
- History ranked by time of day ("3 PM Picks").
- Round + on the right of every row; tapping the row opens the detail.
- Minified plate always visible, one Log button.
- Macro text as "204 Cal • 33P • 7F • 0C": compact and easy to scan.
- Search pill and Log pill pinned at the bottom, within thumb reach.

## Avoid

- The minified plate as "0 / 2476" with no label: the same unlabelled-number problem NutriSA had with "279 kcal". Always say what a number is.
- No meal names. Fine for MacroFactor's audience; NutriSA keeps Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack.
