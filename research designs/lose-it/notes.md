## How logging works

- Add Food from the day, choose the meal, then search, scan, photo or voice.
- Each meal on the Log tab is a card headed "Breakfast: 330 cals".

## Take for NutriSA

- A meal header that includes the meal total ("Breakfast: 330 cals").
- One huge calorie number on a food card ("95 Calories").
- Under or over shown as one word next to the number ("118 Under").

## Avoid

- Barcode scanning has been premium only since October 2022.
- Ads in the log flow (reported by reviewers).
- Bright orange header (NutriSA's tokens have no orange).
