## How logging works

- The meal is the header dropdown on search.
- Results have a circle to tick several at once, then add them together.
- Tabs switch between recipes, foods and recently eaten.

## Take for NutriSA

- "Breakfast ▾" with the date under it as the logger header.
- A keypad fallback on the scanner (type the barcode digits).

## Avoid

- Three lines of serving text and RDI per result: hard to scan.
- Tabs for Recipes / Food / Recently eaten: one list with sections is faster.
