## How logging works

- Tap the + on a meal row to log to that meal.

## Take for NutriSA

- A + on every meal row on the day view, with that meal's kcal.
- Later: a per-meal target ("466 / 566 Cal") once NutriSA has meal splits.

## Avoid

- Mascot and bright accents.
- Slow search and scan flows.
