## How logging works

- Search as you type.
- Up to 5 of your own previous foods that match appear at the top of results, highlighted (help page "Finding Foods").
- It remembers what you typed and which food you picked for it, so the same words find the same food next time.
- You can remove a wrong entry from search history.

## Take for NutriSA

- Your own foods first in results, marked, at most 5.
- Remember query to chosen food.
- Remove from history, for mistakes.
